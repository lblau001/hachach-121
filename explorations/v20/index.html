v20 — the finale

Four options for beat 5. Every frame is 360×640, RTL, on the prototype's own ground; components from explorations/v16/prototype/hachach.css. Hebrew only inside the frames, annotations in English, striped yellow is unwritten copy. Nothing here is implemented. Pick one and I build it.

What is wrong with the one that ships

  • It reads as a document — a result paragraph, a chip row, a link list, two buttons — not as a payoff. The count-up is one line of type in the middle of it.
  • The two buttons work and look poor. That is the thing to solve, not to preserve.

What every option carries

  • The tally counting up, as the visual centre.
  • The player's vote counted as the 121st: ההצעה עברה 63—57. עם הקול שלכם: 64—57
  • The shape-of-the-guess line, the explanation with glossary chips, and the links.
  • Two buttons: primary לסוגיה הבאה › and secondary חזרה למפה when the topic has a second issue; primary חזרה למפה alone when it does not.

A vote board, not the vote board

  • No Knesset emblem, no menorah, no colonnade, no seat plan. Those are protected insignia and were already excluded for trademark reasons. What is drawn is the generic idea of a plenum display: two counters climbing, a bar, an outcome line.

Degradation is drawn, not described

  • 7 of the 11 active issues have no _tally — e3, e4, b3, g1, g3, a2, m3. There is nothing to count. Every option below is drawn twice: with numbers and without.
  • 5 of 11 have no cascade — e3, e4, b3, g3, m3 — so the shape-of-the-guess line has nothing to say either. The no-tally frames drop both.
  • What all 11 do have is vote_result: Tamar's own prose, which on the no-tally issues is the only account of the outcome that exists.

Where this leaves things

The four sit on how much the count-up costs the rest of the beat. V20-1 gives it the most and the content the least; V20-2 the reverse; V20-3 refuses the trade by splitting the beat in two; V20-4 changes what the count-up IS.

  • The buttons. Three of the four use the same pair — a full-width primary and the secondary demoted to a centred underlined link. That is the fix for "they look poor": the old pair was a primary and an outlined .r-b of equal width, which read as two choices of equal weight when one of them is just the way out. V20-1 draws the alternative (side by side) so the pair can be judged.
  • The missing tallies are the real decision. 7 of 11 issues have no _tally, so on 7 of 11 rounds none of these options can count anything. Every frame above is drawn twice for that reason. If those 7 can gain a _tally in data.js, all four options get much stronger; if they cannot, V20-2 is the only one whose degraded state is not a compromise.
  • Nothing here reproduces the Knesset's display board, emblem or colonnade.
