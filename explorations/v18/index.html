v18 — the claim reveal

Five options for the beat-1 reveal. Every frame is 360×640, RTL, on the prototype's own ground; components come from explorations/v16/prototype/hachach.css, the locked handoff sheet. Hebrew appears only inside the frames, every annotation is English, and striped yellow is unwritten copy pending Tamar. Nothing here is implemented. Pick one and I build it.

What is wrong with the one that ships today

  • Too little visible behind the stamp — it reads as a modal takeover with no context.
  • The player's own correctness is never shown; only the true answer is.
  • The coin count disappears. So does the issue title. The whole HUD goes.
  • The CTA is too large, sits too high, and has no chevron.

What every option below therefore carries

  • The true answer — אמת / שקר.
  • A separate mark of whether the player was right. Never the same object as the answer.
  • The coin chip and the issue title, both still on screen.
  • The explanation, with inline glossary terms live.
  • A CTA with a chevron.

The locked rule these are designed against

  • Correctness is coded by colour only — lime #B6E521 for right, magenta #FF3BC0 for surprised. Never by vote direction, never by which word is shown, never by a tick.
  • So the two marks must not be confusable. A player must never read “you were right” as “the claim was true”. Each option below states how it separates them; that is the thing to judge.

Every frame shows the same case: the claim on s1 is false, and the player answered true — so the true answer is שקר and the player was wrong. That is the harder of the two states to draw, because it is the one where the two marks disagree and can be misread for each other.

V18-1 · the stamp lands on the card

Nothing is covered. The claim card stays where it is at full size, the stamp lands on the card exactly as the verdict stamp does at beat 4, and the explanation rises as a sheet over the card's lower half. The correctness mark lives in the chyron slot, which is a different plane from the card entirely.

V18-2 · achromatic stamp, coloured ribbon

Closest to what ships, and the option that fixes it by splitting the one object into two. Today's stamp is coloured by correctness and lettered with the true answer, so a single mark is doing both jobs. Here the stamp face goes achromatic ink and carries the answer alone; a ribbon under it carries the player's outcome and is the only coloured thing on the screen.

V18-3 · two bands: the claim, and you

The two marks are put in two different places rather than two different treatments. The top band is about the claim and is achromatic. The bottom band is about the player, carries their avatar and their own answer, and is the only place colour appears. Nothing in the top band can be misread as being about the player, because the player is not in it.

V18-5 · the reveal is a strip, the card never moves

The maximum-context end of the range, drawn so the set has one. The card stays fully lit and uncovered; the stamp lands as a token in the chyron slot next to the player's own answer, and the explanation opens as a panel over the card's lower third only. The claim sentence is legible the whole time.

Where this leaves things

The five sit on one axis — how much of the beat is a takeover. V18-5 keeps everything and says the least; V18-2 is closest to today and covers the most; V18-1 and V18-4 keep the card and differ on whether the reveal lands on it or turns it; V18-3 throws the card away and shows you against the truth instead. Two build notes that apply whichever is picked:

  • The correctness word is unwritten in all five — every frame stripes it. צדקת / הופתעת are placeholders and the pair has to come from Tamar together, because they are read as a pair.
  • The stamp goes achromatic in all five, which is a change to a shipped component: today stamp(ok, override) colours the disc by correctness and letters it with the true answer. Splitting those is the fix in every option, so it is not a tie-breaker between them — it happens either way.
  • partial (חלקית) is unreachable across all 11 active issues, so no frame draws it. If Tamar's copy ever introduces one, V18-3 is the only layout with room for a third word without re-flowing.
