v21 — the round banner

Five treatments, each drawn with both contents it has to hold: the beat-1 correctness chip and the beats 2–5 pinned vote. Strips are 392px wide on the prototype's own ground, HUD row included so the 8px clearance is visible. Nothing here is implemented. All OPEN.

What is wrong with the one treatment they currently share

  • The band is full width with a lot of empty grey — on beat 1 it is 361px holding one 119px chip, so it is 67% void.
  • The neon outline reads as a container, not as an object: a lit rectangle around mostly nothing looks like a frame waiting to be filled.
  • The two contents have different jobs and the same box. One is a result about the player; the other is the player's position, which is never right or wrong.

Binding on every option

  • ≥8px from the HUD. Already applied in the build regardless of what is picked here.
  • The beat-1 chip must read as the player's result. On a שקר round a lime chip beside a false claim currently reads as approval of the claim — which is why the avatar is in it in every option below.
  • The pinned vote must never look scored. No verdict hue, no tick, no comparison anywhere near it.

The sticker language the rest of the app already speaks. Sized to contents, white die-cut, keyline, flat extrusion, slightly off-square — the same construction as the ask sticker, the map nodes and the modals. This is also the one that makes §5's callout trivial: the tachles callout is specified as a sibling of the banner, and a skewed sticker over the chair is exactly that.

Risk: the skew is charming once and may wear over eleven issues, since this element is on screen for four of the five beats. And two skewed stickers — this and the ask sticker on the card below it — may be one tilt too many in one column.

§5 · the tachles callout, and the move into the cascade

Blocked on the choice above, and only for its edge: the callout is specified as a sibling of the banner, so it inherits whichever fill and accent wins. Everything else — the geometry, the sequence and the timings — is independent and is proposed here. Drawn with B-5's sticker treatment because that is the option under which the callout costs nothing extra.

The callout

  • Sits over the lower part of the chair rather than in a plain plate under it, skewed ~1.6°, same die-cut and keyline as the banner it will become.
  • Copy unchanged: זו הצעת חוק אמיתית. כח”כ ה-121, אתם מצביעים במליאה — ואז נראה איך הצביעו האחרים.
  • It is the same object that becomes the banner — not a plate that fades out and a banner that fades in. That identity is the whole reason the sequence reads as one move.

Proposed timings — 1,720ms end to end

  • 1 · the letters fly — 0→340ms. The chosen chip's label detaches and travels to the callout on --e-in; the two unchosen chips recede to .3 over the first 160ms so nothing else is moving when the letters arrive.
  • 2 · the avatar joins — 180→480ms. Overlaps step 1 by 160ms deliberately. It flies from the HUD's avatar slot, arriving 140ms after the letters so the pair assembles rather than lands together.
  • 3 · the callout resolves — 480→740ms. The framing text cross-fades out over 140ms while the sticker contracts to [avatar] הצבעת: בעד — a width transition on the same element, so it is one object changing rather than two swapping.
  • 4 · it travels to the pin — 740→1,120ms. 380ms on --e-settle, the longest single step, because this is the move the player is meant to follow.
  • 5 · the next screen enters behind it — 900→1,300ms. Starts while the banner is still travelling. The cascade comes up at scale .97 with a 10px blur that clears over 400ms. The banner holds z-index above every layer for the whole transition, including the blur layer — the one thing that must not go wrong.
  • 6 · the tap affordance — from 1,300ms. The blur does not fully clear: it settles at 3px and holds. A [טקסט — תמר] line and a slow 2.4s pulse on the deck say the screen is waiting. Tapping anywhere clears the last 3px over 220ms and arms the cascade.

The two things to get right

  • The banner above every layer. It has to outrank the incoming screen, the blur and the HUD for the whole 1,720ms. In the current DOM the chyron is inside #scRound, which showScreen() hides — so the transition cannot be built by swapping screens underneath it. The banner has to be promoted out of the screen it lives in, or the two screens have to co-exist for the duration. That is a structural change and it is the real cost of §5, not the animation.
  • Step 6 must not read as dead. A fully-cleared blur with nothing moving is a screen that looks finished and isn't. Holding 3px of blur is what keeps it legibly a waiting state, and the pulse is what makes the wait an invitation.

Where this leaves things

  • Four of the five size the banner to its contents. That is the single change that removes the void, and it is independent of fill and accent — worth deciding first.
  • The neon survives in one option only (B-3, as an edge rather than a ring). If it goes entirely, #37C4FF returns to the glossary and is free for other uses.
  • The avatar is in the beat-1 chip in every option. That is not a variable — it is what stops a lime chip beside a שקר claim reading as approval of the claim.
  • §5 is blocked on this. The tachles callout inherits whichever edge treatment is chosen; B-5 makes it a one-line change, B-4 leaves the callout with no family to belong to.
